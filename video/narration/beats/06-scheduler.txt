Stage five. The pods exist, but no node is assigned. The scheduler runs two distinct phases. Filtering: which nodes could run this at all? Requests against allocatable capacity, taints, node affinity, topology constraints, volume topology, free host ports. Nodes that fail are eliminated outright. Then scoring: of the survivors, which is best? Spread, resource balance, preferences. Filtering uses your declared requests, not what your pods are actually using. A cluster can sit at fifteen percent measured CPU and still refuse to schedule, because every pod requested far more than it needs. When a pod is Pending, dashboards showing free capacity are not evidence.
