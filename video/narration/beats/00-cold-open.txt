You type kubectl apply. A few seconds later, traffic reaches your application. In between, at least six independent systems have to agree — and each one can fail in a way that looks exactly like the others. A pod stuck Pending. A pod Running but getting no traffic. A service that resolves but times out. Every one of those is a different subsystem, and telling them apart is most of what senior Kubernetes work actually is. So let's follow one declaration all the way down: from a line of YAML, to a packet arriving at your process.
