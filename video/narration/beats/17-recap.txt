So: desired object, admission and storage, watch and cache, controller queue, scheduler queue and binding, kubelet, CRI, CNI, CSI, EndpointSlice, service, DNS, data plane, application. Every stage asynchronous. Every stage observable. Every stage retryable. And no stage in charge of the one after it. If you can walk that spine out loud, you can place almost any Kubernetes failure on it within about thirty seconds — which is the actual skill being tested when someone asks you how Kubernetes works.
