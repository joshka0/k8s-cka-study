Stage twelve, and we go back up to the control plane. The readiness probe passes, the kubelet reports ready, and the EndpointSlice controller notices. It adds your pod's IP to the Service's EndpointSlice — the object that lists which backends are currently able to serve. This is the mechanical reason a readiness probe controls traffic. It is not magic and it is not local: readiness changes an API object, and that object is what every node's data plane reads. Which also means that if your service has no ready backends, the EndpointSlice is empty, and that single fact explains the outage faster than any packet capture will.
