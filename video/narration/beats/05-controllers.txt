Stage four. Controllers notice — not one controller, several, chained. The Deployment controller sees a Deployment with no matching ReplicaSet and creates one. The ReplicaSet controller sees a ReplicaSet with fewer pods than it wants and creates pods. Each watches the API, compares what exists against what was declared, and takes one idempotent step to close the gap. That comparison is level-based: it asks what should be true now rather than replaying a history of events. So a dropped watch event costs latency, never correctness — the next reconcile recomputes from current state and gets the same answer.
