Stage eleven. The process is alive, and the pod's phase becomes Running. Running is not ready. Running means the process started. Ready means the application will accept traffic — and those are separated on purpose. The startup probe gates the others while a slow application boots. The liveness probe restarts the container if it wedges. The readiness probe does something different: it does not restart anything, it controls whether you receive traffic at all. Getting these confused is how a slightly slow dependency turns into an endless restart loop.
