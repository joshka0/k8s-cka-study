Stage three. The object is written to etcd, and a write is not durable the moment the leader receives it. etcd is a Raft consensus group: a majority of members must commit the entry before the write is acknowledged. Floor of N over two, plus one. Three members tolerate one failure, and adding a fourth buys nothing — quorum rises with it. Five is the next size that helps. Your pod now exists only as a record in a database. Nothing is running. Nothing has been scheduled. You have declared intent, and that is all.
