Stage six, and it is smaller than it sounds. The scheduler does not place the pod on the node. It does not contact the node. It does not start anything. Its entire output is a binding — one API write that sets the pod's nodeName field. The decision is now a fact in the database, and the scheduler moves to the next pod. Everything that follows is somebody else's job, triggered only because a kubelet was watching for pods with its own name on them.
