Stage two: the gauntlet. The request is authenticated — who is this? Then authorised — may they do this? RBAC lives here, and it only grants permissions; it never establishes identity. Then mutating admission, which may change your object: injecting a sidecar, adding a default. Then validating admission, which may reject it. Mutation runs first, so what gets validated is the final object. Only then is it written to etcd and watchers notified. Every gate is a separate reason your apply can fail, and they fail with different messages. When RBAC allows something and it is still rejected, think admission webhooks, schema validation, quota, immutable fields.
