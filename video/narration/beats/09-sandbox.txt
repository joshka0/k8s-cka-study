Stage eight. The kubelet calls the container runtime over CRI, a gRPC contract, and the boundary the kubelet actually speaks across. The runtime creates the pod sandbox first, before any of your containers. The sandbox holds the namespaces your containers will share — network, IPC — anchored by a tiny pause container that runs no application code. The network has to belong to something before a container can join it, so you can have a healthy runtime and a pod with no networking.
